Mikko & Irene

Finnish summer brings the best opportunities for international enjoyment. Sun, nature, a calm breeze under a tree, the perfect temperature with the perfect people around. Irene is from Taiwan and Mikko from Finland — they met in the Netherlands, where they still live. But they chose Finland to celebrate their love, with a guest list of very special people travelling from Asia and North America.

Luckily, the weather was on their side for exactly as long as they needed it — right after the coverage was done, a huge storm rolled in. Classic Finnish summer! By then everyone was already inside, enjoying drinks and delicious food in a beautiful place.
