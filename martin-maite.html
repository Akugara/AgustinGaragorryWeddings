Martín & Maite

When COVID restrictions were limiting gatherings and postponing weddings, Maite and Martín saw an opportunity. They chose to celebrate their love with an intimate group—something truly special.

Their unique celebration brought together two gatherings: first with elder family members, then with their circle of friends. Perfect weather blessed the day, and years later, I can confirm their love story continues beautifully with a new member of their family.
